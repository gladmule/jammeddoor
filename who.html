WHO IS JAMMED DOOR?

Jammed door is run by me. I like reading 'about me' pages because they always teeter on the brink of awkwardly serious 3rd person professional or an abrupt few lines that leave you with more questions than you thought you had. Which is a good ay to go in my opinion. It's difficult to say who you are... really. I'm really not entirely sure what to write here. It's a little self indulgent... but it sees like nobody is holding a reader captive here so here it is: I guess I'm currently existing somewhere between film, music and philosophy. But we always exist somewhere between life and death, that doesn't say much. I'm doing some documentary film, doing some fiction film, playing a bit of music, making some music, writing a bit. I am interested in creating a space primarily as a concentration of the ideas that I have toyed with for the past years. I'm interested in stuff I haven't seen or heard yet so I couldn't tell you. Then again there are some things I know that interest me, but the question arises whether they are just familiar or if I really really find them interesting. There's the question of wether familiarity is necessary for depth or if it only jades your perception. I like weird jazz.. but not too jazzy... and not too cerebral. I like people calling many things jazz as a half-joke. I've always really liked the term fake jazz. It suggests the existence of real jazz, and positions whatever as fake jazz as definitely not the real thing. Where is fake classical? Why is fake jazz somehow so inviting? Fake rock only exists in the movies. I'm interested in improv music, an interest that is perhaps fading slowly from the disillusionment of the homogeneity of noise. But that is another topic.
